Project I.G.I. Stealth Tactics for Difficult Missions

Project I.G.I. rewards patience and observation. A little preparation can make difficult infiltration missions much more manageable.

Last updated: 30 October 2026 · Gameplay · 4 min read

Before crossing an open area, pause and look for patrol routes, elevated positions and available cover. Avoid treating every encounter as a direct firefight: the safest route may be a longer route that keeps you out of sight.

Use optics to assess distant threats and choose engagements deliberately. Keep track of ammunition and equipment, and retreat to cover when a position becomes unsafe. Mission objectives often reward understanding the environment rather than rushing forward.

These are general gameplay suggestions; the best approach depends on the mission layout, your equipment and the difficulty setting.
