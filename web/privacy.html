Privacy Policy

This policy explains the browser-side storage and third-party services used by this fan project.

Game files and browser storage

When you choose a game folder, the browser port copies the files into storage associated with this website's origin. The import flow is designed to keep those files on your device rather than upload them to the site's server. Browser storage may be cleared by you, browser settings or storage pressure. Keep your own backup.

Information collected by the website

The static website does not ask you to create an account. The current deployment should not connect analytics until the owner configures an approved analytics property and any required consent mechanism. Technical hosting logs may be processed by the hosting provider to deliver and secure the site.

Third-party services and advertising

Advertising code may load third-party resources when ad slots are enabled. Those providers may process request metadata under their own policies. This page must be updated if analytics, comments, advertising vendors or other third-party services are enabled or changed.

Contact and rights requests

Use the contact page for technical or privacy questions. A verified public contact email will be added when the site owner supplies one.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the site changes. Last updated: 30 October 2026.
